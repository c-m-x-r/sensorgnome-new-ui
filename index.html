This receiver is part of the MOTUS Wildlife Tracking System — a hemispheric network of automated radio stations operated by Birds Canada, spanning North America from the Gulf of Mexico to the Arctic. Attached to the antenna is a software-defined radio tuned to 166.380 MHz, listening for coded Lotek NanoTags carried by migratory birds, bats, and insects. Each tag transmits a silent burst of four pulses every few seconds; the timing between pulses encodes a unique animal ID via pulse-position modulation. Confirmed detections are compressed, timestamped with GPS-disciplined precision, and uploaded to motus.org where they join a continental movement record used by ecologists to study migration corridors, stopover ecology, and species range shifts.
